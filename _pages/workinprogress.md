## Under review

**Military Alliance Portfolios and Civil War Onset: A Global Counterfactual Analysis**
<br>
Single-authored. In this paper, I study how states' military alliance portfolios relate to the onset of civil war using a global counterfactual analysis.

**The night before or the morning after: Coup risk, failed coup attempts, and minister removals in autocracies**
<br>
With Christian Oswald, Sonja Häffner, Marco N. Binetti, and Christoph Dworschak. We investigate whether coup risk is a better indicator for minister removals than failed coup attempts.

## In progress

**The Moral Hazard of Alliances: External Guarantees, Repression, and Protest**
<br>
Single-authored. In this paper, I study how external security guarantees from alliances relate to government repression and protest.

**Friend or Foe: Using Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis to Measure Relations between Member States at the UN General Assembly**
<br>
Single-authored. In this paper, I apply stance detection to UN General Assembly speeches. Explore the data in the [UNGA stance app](/portfolio/unga/).

**The Impact of Sanctions Imposition Coordination: A Time-Varying Measure of EU and US Sanctions Coordination in the Cases of Russia, Iran, and North Korea**
<br>
Single-authored. In this paper, I try to measure sanctions coordination between the US and the EU using NLP techniques.

**Two Sides of the Same Coin: Forced Resettlement and Mass Killing**
<br>
With Christoph Dworschak. In this paper, we investigate how countries apply different strategies of indiscriminate targeting.
